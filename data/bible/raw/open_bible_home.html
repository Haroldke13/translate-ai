God’s Word, Freely Received, Freely Given

Open.Bible is your online hub for free Bible texts, audio Bibles, and maps — all freely released for use under Creative Commons licensing. No hassle. No red tape. No waiting to engage your community with God’s Word. Start creating today!

Why creative commons

It’s all about getting Scripture to more people faster than ever before.

Open licensing also accelerates print and digital publishing so there’s no wait for audiences to engage with God’s Word.

This radical alternative to the traditional licensing model makes it easier, cheaper, and faster than ever for Bible translators and publishers to create life-changing Scripture derivatives.
